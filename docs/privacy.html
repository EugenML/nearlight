Nearlight – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Nearlight is an arcade flight game. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

Data we collect

None. Nearlight does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Network

The app makes no network requests of its own. All graphics and music are generated on the device, and the app works fully offline; only the optional Game Center scores above go through Apple.

Device features

The app uses the device's audio output for music and may use haptic feedback (vibration) during wormhole jumps. It does not access your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, the app sends your score – the distance you flew in light-years, your flight time and the number of galaxies you reached – to Apple's Game Center leaderboards, so they can appear next to your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; the app itself does not collect, store or share this or any other personal data, and you can play without Game Center.

Children

The app contains no content or features that collect information from anyone, including children.
